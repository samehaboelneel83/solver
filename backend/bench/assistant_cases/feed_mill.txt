Rules: the ingredients put into a feed add up to exactly the tons of that feed. In each feed, protein is at least its minimum percentage of the feed's weight, fibre is at most its maximum percentage, and calcium is between its minimum and maximum percentage. Fish meal is at most 5 percent of the weight of any feed. Across both feeds we cannot use more of an ingredient than is available. Tons can be fractions. Goal: the lowest total cost of the ingredients used.

Everything you need is above: please propose the model without further questions, then solve it.
