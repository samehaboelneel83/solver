# The steps before a solve, measured: escalate only when the solver alone settles nothing (9 October 2026)

`python -m bench.pipeline --time-limit 30 --workers 8` in a worker of the PC stack (8 threads), 23 instances: the
benchmark families where a step can apply, and the models the steps were built on (facility location with and
without capacities, lot sizing, multi-item lot sizing, capacitated network design, a network that chooses its
places). Each arm is timed whole, the step's own time included, and scored as the learnt choices score a run:
seconds to a proof, or for an unproven run the time allowed times (2 + gap). `strengthen`, `race`, `start` and
`all` are from the first run of the day; `base` and `escalate` were run again together (the new `base` agrees with
the first to within a few percent everywhere but network-prize-10-1, 7.2 s then and 11.4 s now: CP-SAT's own
run-to-run spread on that model).

## Every step on, always (the arms before `escalate`)

Zero wrong answers, but every step costs more than it saves on the models a solver proves alone in seconds, and
those are most of them: the implied rows made 11 of 13 slower (geometric mean 1.9x), the start 13 of 16 (3.4x),
everything together 12 of 13 (5.1x). The gains are real but only on the hard instances: ufl-100x400-1 ends at a
3.0% gap instead of 7.1%, network-design-16-2 at 5.4% instead of 7.7%.

## Escalate: the solver alone first

`escalate` is what a run now does (`solve.probe_first`, on by default): the solver tries the model as it is for a
fifth of the time allowed (2 to 30 s). A proof there is the answer. Otherwise the steps that apply run, with what
it found as their start and the time left.

Of the 16 instances where a step applies, 13 were proved inside the probe and paid nothing for the steps (within
the 10% noise of base). The two hard ones keep their gains (ufl-100x400-1: 3.0% against 7.1%; network-design-16-2:
5.4% against 7.7%). The cost is on a model that just misses the probe: network-prize-10-1 was not proved in its
6 s, and the solve after the steps starts its search again (21.6 s against 11.4 s). network-design-12-1 ends at
1.9% against 1.6%. Geometric mean 1.05x against 5.07x for every step always: the default is escalation.

The probe is skipped where it cannot help: when a lane of the run's own takes the model (min-cost flow, a
matching, a spanning tree, a horizon, a decomposition, sampled futures, a search, alternatives), when a race or
portfolio of solvers is asked for, for fronts, and for solvers that take no start. A run records it as
`probe_first_run` ("Solver alone first" on the run page); `solve.probe_first` off restores the steps first.
