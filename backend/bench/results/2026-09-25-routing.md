# Vehicle routing: exact rows, and the routing search as their start -- 2026-09-25 (queue R15b)

`python -m bench.routing --time-limit 120`. Capacitated vehicle routing on
random points (a depot in the middle, stops with demands 1-9, equal
vehicles with 30% spare capacity, whole distances, least total distance),
written with the new `route` rule. CP-SAT (the rules' choice) solved the
exact rows, sandboxed as a run, from nothing and from `app.solve.routing`'s
start; the start's own routes are shown beside them. In this run the search
took a fifth of the time (24 s) at every size; it now takes at most half a
second a stop (7 stops: 4 s), which found each optimum here well inside it.

**Verdict: `solve.routing_start` on (migration 0063).**

- **7 and 15 stops:** CP-SAT proves the optimum either way, and the routing
  search had already found it (254, 497).
- **30 stops:** from the start CP-SAT ends at 554 (the search's own); from
  nothing, at 561. Neither is proven; the gaps differ only in how far each
  bound got in the time left.
- **60 stops:** CP-SAT runs out of 4 GB on the exact rows either way (60 x 60
  x 8 visits and loads); a run keeps the search's routes (930) as its
  answer -- `feasible`, claiming nothing.

Correctness is pinned apart from the bench: `tests/test_route.py` checks the
exact rows against every split and order of the stops on six small
instances, loads on and off, and a loop away from the depot is refused by
the rows.
