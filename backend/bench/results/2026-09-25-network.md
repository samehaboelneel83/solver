# Networks solved as networks -- 2026-09-25 (queue R15a)

`python -m bench.network --time-limit 120`. Four network families written
as ordinary models (transport: plants to customers, continuous; assignment:
people to tasks, yes/no; max flow and shortest path on a square grid,
continuous, capacities written as rules). Each solved by the network lane
(OR-Tools min-cost flow, in-process), and sandboxed as a run by the rules'
choice and by HiGHS. Times include the sandbox's start (about 0.05 s for
GLOP, 0.6 s for HiGHS's own process).

**Verdict: `solve.network` on (migration 0062), for networks with
whole-number decisions.** 16 of 16 agree with the proven optimum. On the
assignment, the one whole-number family, the rules send it to CP-SAT: 2.6 s
at 150 x 150 against 0.13 s, and at 400 x 400 CP-SAT runs out of 4 GB
where the lane proves it in 1.0 s (HiGHS 11.7 s). On the continuous families
GLOP proves every size within 2 s, and the lane's 4x is not worth what it
drops: min-cost flow gives no shadow prices, and the Runs page shows GLOP's.
So a continuous network stays with the LP solvers.
