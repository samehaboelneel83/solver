## Reading

`bench.download_miplib` fetches, on HiGHS and the MILP wrapper (SCIP
through OR-Tools), 60 s each, 8 threads. Every answer is checked against
MIPLIB's published solution file as well as against the other backend: a
proven optimum must equal the published one, and an unproven answer must
not beat it. **0 wrong.**

The two instances either backend proves in a minute agree with MIPLIB to
the unit, which is the evidence the MPS reader and both adapters read a
real model correctly (objective constant included). The other eight are
beyond a minute for both, as expected for this set; where the answer is
already optimal but unproven (pk1, neos5, markshare_4_0 on the MILP
wrapper) the gap is the lower bound's, not the answer's. This lane
measures solver-parameter techniques (Phase 13); the families measure the
rest.
