# The probe race against the rule choice — 2026-09-24

`python -m bench.race --sizes M,L --instances 3 --time-limit 30` (queue 17b).
Each nightly family instance with more than one admissible solver and above
the size floor: the rule's choice alone for 30 s, against the race (every
admissible solver probed at once for 3 s with the threads shared out, the
first proof ending the race, the best continued for the rest). A solve
without a proof is charged 30 s.

**Verdict: leave off** (`solve.probe` defaults off, migration 0048). The
race is 2x faster on rota_rates (SCIP wins its probes) and never changes an
optimum, but where no probe proves the model (facility at size L) the winner
starts again from scratch: the probe seconds are pure overhead, and each
probe ran on a share of the threads -- 3.0 s -> 6.3 s, 7.1 s -> 14.3 s. It
stays available per problem or domain. The next thing to try is carrying the
winner's probe forward (its incumbent as a warm start, or not restarting it)
instead of solving again.
