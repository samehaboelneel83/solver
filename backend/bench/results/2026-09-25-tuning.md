# Tuning search per family — 2026-09-25

`python -m bench.tune --time-limit 20` (queue R10). For each family, the
backend the rules choose for it and **every** combination of that backend's
whitelisted options (`app.solve.params.WHITELIST`: 9 for CP-SAT, 6 for
HiGHS) -- few enough to try all, so no sampler (Optuna's TPE, as the roadmap
proposed) is needed or added. Each is scored on training instances (M and L,
instances 0 and 1) by the shifted geometric mean of its time, a solve
without a proof charged the limit; the best is kept only if, against the
defaults, it is at least 10% faster on an instance it never saw (L, instance
2, two seeds), never twice as slow, and never a different optimum.

**Tuned: one family.** Rota on CP-SAT: `linearization_level=0` and
`symmetry_level=0` -- 0.74 s -> 0.39 s on training, 0.65 s -> 0.50 s (22%)
held out, same optimum. To use it on a rota-shaped domain or problem, set

    solve.solver_params = cp-sat.linearization_level=0, cp-sat.symmetry_level=0
    solve.tuned_from    = 2026-09-25 bench/results/2026-09-25-tuning.md

Nothing is set on any live domain by this: the evidence is the bench's rota
family, and a domain's owner decides whether their problem is shaped like it.

**Defaults stand everywhere else.** On every other family the training
winner did not hold on the held-out instance (rota_teams came closest: 9%,
under the 10% bar), or the defaults were best on training
(facility_regions). The platform-wide enabled option (SCIP's fast
presolve, 2026-09-23) is unchanged.
