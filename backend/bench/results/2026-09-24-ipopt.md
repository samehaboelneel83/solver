# IPOPT after SCIP on continuous nonlinear models — 2026-09-24

`python -m bench.ipopt --time-limit 20` (queue R6), in `solver-backend-test`
(casadi 3.8.1, IPOPT inside its wheel). Two nonconvex families at 10, 50 and
200 decisions: `sines` (a sine per decision and a cosine of neighbouring
pairs, under a budget; NLP) and `bilinear` (products of neighbours less a
quadratic, under a budget; QP). SCIP alone for 20 s; then, as a run with
`solve.local_fallback` does, IPOPT for 5 s from SCIP's answer.

**Verdict: leave off** (`solve.local_fallback` defaults off, migration 0055).
SCIP proves every bilinear instance within the time. On `sines` it ends with
an answer and a wide bound at every size, and IPOPT started from that answer
finds it already a local optimum: the one "improvement" (sines 200) was
211.829563 -> 211.829564, rounding -- the fallback now ignores a change
smaller than 1e-7 of the answer. No answer ever passed SCIP's bound.

IPOPT stays available by name for any continuous nonlinear model (its answer
recorded `local`, shown with the warning, never called optimal) and as the
fallback per problem or domain. Its case is a model where SCIP finds no
answer at all -- which these families, sized as the platform builds them,
never reached. Next to try: several starting points (multi-start) rather
than one.
