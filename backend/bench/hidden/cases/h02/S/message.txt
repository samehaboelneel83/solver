Rules (all hard): a project is either funded in full (all its yearly outlays) or not at all. Budget: money not spent in a year carries over to the following years, but we cannot spend ahead of budget, so for every year the outlays of funded projects from the first year up to that year are at most the budgets from the first year up to that year. Projects sharing an exclusive_group are alternatives: at most one of each group (a blank group means no restriction). A project may be funded only if every project it requires is funded. Each category listed in the minimums has at least that many funded projects. Goal: the largest total NPV of the funded projects.
