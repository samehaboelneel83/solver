Please build this in a new workspace called "{workspace}". Call the problem "Term timetable". Our college must give each of 60 courses next term a teacher and a weekly time slot (one of 10 slots; each course meets once a week in its slot).

The data are in the attached files: courses.csv (cohort, room type needed, weekly hours), teachers.csv (maximum weekly hours), qualifications.csv (the only teacher-course pairs allowed, with the teacher's preference from 1 = dislikes to 5 = loves), slots.csv (whether a slot is a late evening slot), unavailable.csv (teacher-slot pairs the teacher cannot do), rooms.csv (rooms of each type available in every slot).

Rules (all hard): every course gets exactly one teacher, who must be qualified for it (listed in the qualifications), and exactly one slot. A teacher's courses add up to at most the teacher's maximum weekly hours (a course counts its weekly hours). A teacher teaches at most one course in a slot and nothing in a slot where the teacher is unavailable. Two courses of the same cohort are never in the same slot. In every slot, the courses needing a room type are at most the rooms of that type. Goal: the largest score = the sum of the preferences of the chosen teacher-course pairs minus 2 points for every course placed in a late slot. Report the teacher and the slot of every course, the total score, each teacher's weekly hours, the courses in late slots, and the rooms used per slot.

Everything you need is here: please propose the model without further questions, then solve it.
