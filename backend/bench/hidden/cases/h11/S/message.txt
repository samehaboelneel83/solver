The day is split into 6 periods of 4 hours, in the order given. Rules (all hard): water moves only along the listed pipes, in the listed direction, and a pipe carries at most its maximum m3 in a period. A source sends out at most its maximum m3 per period, in total over its pipes. Each zone must receive exactly its demand in every period (zones do not pass water on). A tank's level at the end of a period is its level at the end of the previous period (its start level for the first period) plus what flows in minus what flows out during the period; that level must stay between the tank's minimum and maximum at the end of every period, and at the end of the last period every tank must hold at least its start level. Goal, as small as possible, in EGP: production cost (m3 sent out by a source times its production cost per m3) plus pumping energy cost (m3 through a pipe times its pumping kWh per m3 times the period's tariff per kWh).
