Please build this in a new workspace called "{workspace}". Call the problem "Material purchasing". Our plant must buy next month's 25 raw materials from 8 suppliers that offer quantity discounts.

The data are in the attached files: materials.csv (required units and volume per unit), suppliers.csv (order cost, minimum order value and maximum units per supplier), price_breaks.csv (the quantity-discount price list per supplier and material).

Rules (all hard): for every material we buy at least the required units in total (buying more is allowed). A material can be bought only from suppliers that quote it in the price list. Quantities are whole units. Discounts are all-units: the unit price of an order line (one supplier, one material) is the price of the highest from_units row that the line's quantity reaches, and that price applies to every unit of the line. A supplier we buy anything from charges its order cost once, and the value of everything we buy from it (quantity times unit price, before the order cost) must be at least its min_order_value. A supplier sells us at most max_units units in total over all materials. Everything bought must fit in our store: the total volume (units times volume per unit) is at most 14,575 m3. Goal, as small as possible, in EGP: the value of all order lines plus the order costs of the suppliers used. Report the quantity bought per supplier and material with its unit price, the suppliers used, the total cost split into material value and order costs, where we buy above the requirement to reach a discount, and the storage volume used.

Everything you need is here: please propose the model without further questions, then solve it.
